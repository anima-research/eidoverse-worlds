Credits & licences

eidoverse-worlds runs on other people's generous work. These are the projects whose code, data or designs ship in this client or shaped it, with their licences. Each item's licence text is at the bottom of the page or linked from it.

Code and data the client loads

three.js MIT
The renderer. © 2010–2025 three.js authors. github.com/mrdoob/three.js
three-vrm, three-vrm-animation, three-vrm-materials-mtoon MIT
Avatars (VRM), their animation and toon shading. © 2019 pixiv Inc. github.com/pixiv/three-vrm
three-mesh-bvh MIT
Fast raycasts and collision against meshes. © 2018 Garrett Johnson. github.com/gkjohnson/three-mesh-bvh
Rapier (rapier3d-compat) Apache-2.0
Physics. © 2020 Dimforge EURL. rapier.rs
ammo.js / Bullet Physics zlib
Body physics (ragdolls). Bullet © 2003–2014 Erwin Coumans; ammo.js © Alon Zakai and contributors. github.com/kripken/ammo.js
Basis Universal transcoder Apache-2.0
Compressed (KTX2) textures, as bundled with three.js. © 2019–2024 Binomial LLC. github.com/BinomialLLC/basis_universal
Draco decoder Apache-2.0
Compressed meshes, as bundled with three.js and served from this server. © Google LLC. github.com/google/draco
Phosphor Icons MIT
The fill-weight icons (path data copied from @phosphor-icons/core). © 2023 Phosphor Icons (Helena Zhang, Tobias Fried). phosphoricons.com
piper-tts-web MIT
In-browser text-to-speech. © Mintplex Labs. github.com/Mintplex-Labs/piper-tts-web
ONNX Runtime Web MIT
Runs the speech models. © Microsoft Corporation. github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime
piper-phonemize (rhasspy) and piper-wasm (Diffusion Studio) MIT
Turn text into sounds for the speech models. rhasspy/piper-phonemize, diffusion-studio/piper-wasm
eSpeak NG data GPL-3.0
The pronunciation dictionaries inside vendor/piper/piper_phonemize.data, distributed unmodified as built by piper-wasm 1.0.0. Corresponding source: espeak-ng/espeak-ng (data and library) and diffusion-studio/piper-wasm (the build). Full licence: GPL-3.0 text; provenance and digests: NOTICE. It runs as a separate data file loaded at runtime; nothing else here is derived from it.
Speech voices
Not shipped here. A voice is fetched from Hugging Face when you choose it, or loaded from a file you provide. Each voice carries its own licence, in its model card.

Designs we learned from

Godot Engine MIT
The edit mode's inspector follows Godot's designs (EditorSpinSlider scrubbing, the property dispatcher, multi-node editing) and the components follow Godot's shapes (lights, impulses). © 2014–present Godot Engine contributors; © 2007–2014 Juan Linietsky, Ariel Manzur. godotengine.org
Basis (BasisVR) MIT
The VR client follows Basis's patterns: warming both render variants at load, the soft swap, scaling the avatar to the player, the head chop, voice transport. © 2024 Luke B Doolan. github.com/BasisVR/Basis
